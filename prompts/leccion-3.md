# Prompts de imágenes · Lección 3

Formato horizontal 16:9. Pásamelas por el chat y las coloco.

## 1. Los cuatro mundos (`leccion3-mundos.webp`)
Sección 5, después de la tabla de colores.

> Four luminous horizontal realms stacked vertically like the floors of a cosmic tower: at the top a realm of pure white fire, below it a realm of deep blue water and flowing light, below that a realm of shifting violet air filled with images and clouds, and at the bottom a solid earthly landscape in citrine, olive, russet and black; a single beam of light descends through all four realms, becoming denser as it goes down, mystical engraving style with soft watercolor, no text, no letters

## 2. El Ritual Menor del Pentagrama (`leccion3-lbrp.webp`)
Sección 13, al final.

> A calm figure in a simple dark robe standing with arms outstretched in the shape of a cross at the center of a candlelit room, surrounded by a circle of white light at chest height, with four large glowing blue five-pointed stars (upright, one point up) floating in the air at the four cardinal points, and a small golden six-pointed star shining on the figure's chest; four tall winged angelic presences made of light stand behind each star, one in yellow and violet, one in red and green, one in blue and orange, one in earthy olive and black; serene and protective atmosphere, fine ink lines and watercolor, no text

## 3. El Árbol de la Vida (`leccion3-arbol.webp`)
Sección 15, antes de los ejercicios.

> A majestic tree of light whose branches and roots form the Kabbalistic Tree of Life: ten glowing spheres arranged in three vertical pillars connected by luminous paths, the top sphere brilliant white, then grey and black, blue and scarlet, a golden sun-like sphere in the center, emerald and orange, violet, and at the roots a sphere divided into four earthy colours; a faint dark circle in the gap below the top three spheres; deep night sky background with stars, Renaissance hermetic engraving style with gold leaf accents, no text, no letters
